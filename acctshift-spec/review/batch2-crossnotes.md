## Amendments to batch2-decisions.md (these win over both the sheet and the notes above)
- `exec` never prompts. `run` (the interactive phase-2 wrapper) never prompts during pre-launch credential handling; it MAY ask its §16 post-exit question ("switch and resume?") only when stdin is a TTY and ACCTSHIFT_NONINTERACTIVE is not 1.
- `state.agySlot.owner` holds the agy account `id`, not its name. `wrongAccount.expected/found` are emails.
