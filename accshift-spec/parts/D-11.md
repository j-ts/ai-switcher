
## D11. Name and scope changes (owner decisions, 2026-10-10)

- **Name: `accshift`** (replaces the working name `acctshift`, which was hard to pronounce). Every identifier follows: the binary, the `ACCSHIFT_*` variables, the `j-ts/accshift` repository, the `accshift_test` build tag and the LimitShift helper names.
- **Gemini CLI is out of scope.** v1 has no `gemini` adapter (§1.3, §8.4). LimitShift keeps running gemini runners unwrapped, under every `accountRotation` (§15.4). The research stays in R06 §1. Adding gemini later means a new adapter plus the removed verification items (formerly V-G1…V-G4).
- agy (Google Antigravity CLI) stays in scope. It keeps its files under `~/.gemini` and uses the keychain service `gemini`, which is why that word still appears in agy facts.
